# Internly asset direction

## Style Prompt
Friendly soft 3D characters with matte fabric, warm olive skin, expressive brown eyes, rounded forms, and soft light from the upper left. A recurring curly-haired student in a blue sweatshirt and a recruiter with a dark low bun and navy blazer connect the pack. Art is isolated on true transparency, without text, logos, scenery, or claims.

## Colors
- Royal blue: #2563EB — clothing and graphic accents.
- Midnight navy: #0A1738 — recruiter clothing and dark preview backgrounds.
- Pale blue-white: #F6F8FC — props and MP4 background.
- White: #FFFFFF — graphic details.
- Soft blue: #93B4FA — secondary accents.

## Typography
The website uses Space Grotesk for headings and Inter for body text. These are context only: deliverable illustrations and loops contain no text. The preview gallery uses locally available system fonts.

## Motion
Six-second single-scene loops, 30 fps, silent. Keep character pixels still. Animate only separate bubbles, dots, checkmark, and a restrained blue confetti burst. Use deterministic GSAP timelines with finite durations. Overlays are hidden at the first and final frames for a seamless repeat. No flashes or sudden camera moves.

## What NOT to Do
- No limb or facial deformation.
- No neon effects, oversized confetti storms, or camera shake.
- No words, ratings, logos, or claims baked into art.
- No opaque backgrounds in PNG or WebM deliverables.
- No website changes.
