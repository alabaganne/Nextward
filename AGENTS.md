# Nextward

Shared notes for anyone working on this repo, people or coding tools. `CLAUDE.md` and the Cursor rule point here.

## What Nextward is

- A job board for **IT students and professionals everywhere**: internships, PFE projects, graduate roles and jobs in IT, **around the world**. It is not only for people in Tunisia; copy speaks to everyone.
- Companies post internships and jobs, review applications and message candidates. Admin is an internal role, not an audience.
- The public site is in **English and French** (French pages live under `/fr`).
- Listings come from company posts and from approved sources imported every day (see `README.md`).

## Decisions

- **Name:** the product is **Nextward**. It was called Internly before (and Stagi.tn before that). Don't use the old names in code, copy or docs. Some asset packs say "Nextwand"; that is a typo, not a new name.
- **Scope:** Nextward is no longer students-and-internships only. Copy, features and docs must cover both students and professionals, and both internships and jobs.
- **IT only:** Nextward is for IT people. Listings cover software, web and mobile development, data and AI, DevOps and cloud, cybersecurity, networks and systems, IT support, QA, UI/UX, embedded systems and business intelligence. Every import keeps to this (`importer.scope`, the `focus.tech` group in `config/importer.php`); listings outside it are rejected with "Outside IT scope." Admins can still approve one by hand. Non-IT listings imported before this rule close on their own after 60 days.
- **Global copy:** public pages, page titles, the FAQ and the footer don't frame Nextward around Tunisia. Tunisia stays only where it is a fact: blog posts, the privacy law and the PFE explanation. No "Remote from Tunisia" or "In Tunisia" badges, tabs or chips.
- **Opportunity filters:** tabs All / Internships / Jobs, then one row: Field, Skills, Type, Country, Work mode, Experience, Posted and an **Open worldwide** toggle. No "More filters" panel. Skills lists the catalogue skills on the matching listings, most used first, with a search box; picking several shows roles that ask for any of them (`skills[]`), and saved searches keep them. Older links and saved searches keep their other filters as removable chips. Under the heading the page shows how many listings are open now and how many older ones are archived. The old `/opportunities/tunisia`, `/remote-from-tunisia` and `/relocation` pages redirect to the matching filters.
- **Who can apply:** each listing has `applicant_scope`: `anywhere` (people in any country can apply), `listed` (only `eligible_countries`), `local` (must already be allowed to work in the job's country, like "US work authorization required") or `unknown`. Open worldwide shows `anywhere` listings and never one that excludes Tunisia. `remote_eligibility` (remote from Tunisia) is still kept, worked out from it, for alerts and recommendations.
- **Listing dates:** each listing keeps when it was posted (`posted_at`: the source's date, or when an employer publishes it; `first_posted_at` keeps the first one across reposts) and when it was added to Nextward (`added_at`: the first time it goes live, never changed later). The listing page shows both under Source and dates ("Not given by the source" in light grey when there is no posted date); cards show the posted date, or the added date when there is none.
- **Listing details:** the "Location and who can apply" card lists the work locations, or shows "Not specified" in light grey when the source gives none (location entries with no place in them don't count).
- **AI import review:** the AI reads new listings in batches (`OPPORTUNITY_AI_BATCH`, 10 per call) before they are added. It knows our fields, every skill in the system (the catalogue plus skills admins add) and the filter values, and sets field, kind, seniority, work mode, contract types, programme, skills (only from that list), who can apply, countries, relocation and visa. It also answers whether someone living in Tunisia can apply: a role open to everyone that leaves Tunisia out gets TN excluded so it never shows under Open worldwide, and a remote role open to a region with Tunisia (Africa, MENA, EMEA...) counts as reachable from Tunisia. It also judges quality: scams, pay-to-apply, commission-only, empty or spam listings are rejected as "Low quality" (admins can still publish them). Translations are batched too, a few listings per call. `opportunities:review` fills older listings on demand.
- **AI review of employer posts:** when a company posts a role or changes its title or description, the same AI review runs in the background (`ReviewPostedOpportunity`). It only fills what the company left empty (skills up to the form's 12, field, experience, contract types, programme, who can apply, relocation, visa); anything the company chose stays, and the company can remove what the AI added. It does not moderate employer posts.
- **Skills:** the skills catalogue is the list listings are tagged with (`skills` in `config/importer.php`, about 700 IT skills across web, mobile, data and AI, cloud and DevOps, networks, security, testing, design and embedded), seeded by `SkillSeeder`. Keep it to IT skills, and give names that are also common words a pattern so they are not tagged by mistake (for example `C`, `R`, `Chef` or `Apache`). Admins can search and edit skills under Skills. Stay under about 850 skills: the AI picks from all of them in one list.
- **Imports admin:** a sidebar group with one page per job: Sources (with add and edit pages), Review queue, Reports, Employer claims, and Runs & health. Moderating or merging a listing has its own page, opened from the review queue or a report. Sources has a **Fetch new listings** button (10, 25, 50 or 100): it does what `opportunities:fetch` does, in the background on the queue, one source per job (`FetchNewOpportunities`), skipping job lead boards. Only one fetch runs at a time; the page shows its progress and the listings it added.
- **One candidate role:** everyone looking for work is a **Candidate** ("Candidat"). There is no student, graduate or professional choice anywhere; experience and preferences say the rest. Accounts are candidates, companies or admins.
- **Names in the code:** listings are `Opportunity` (table `opportunities`, `kind` is `internship` or `job`) and people looking for work are `Candidate` (table `candidates`, `userable_type` is `candidate`). The old `Internship`/`Student` names and tables were renamed in October 2026 (`2026_10_18_170000_rename_internships_and_students`); old `/internships/...` and `/students/...` links redirect. Use "opportunity" and "candidate" in code. In copy a listing is a "role" ("offre" in French), never an "opportunity" (owner, 27 October 2026); URLs keep `/opportunities`. "Internship" stays only as a listing kind.
- **Honest content:** no made-up numbers, testimonials or live activity. A company is only called verified when Nextward found and checked its own website (see Verified companies); the badge never means the employer has an account or was vetted in person.
- **Listings in two languages:** every listing has an English and a French version. The original text and its language are never changed; AI translates into the other site language (both, when the original is in another language) without rewriting or "improving" the employer's text. Old listings keep working as they are and are translated over time, newest first, by `opportunities:translate`; closed listings are not translated. Details in `SEO.md`.
- **Privacy:** fonts (Poppins) are self-hosted in `resources/fonts/`. Don't add outside fonts, scripts or trackers without updating the privacy policy (`resources/js/Pages/Privacy.vue`).
- **Sign-up sources:** shared links can carry `?ref=<slug>`. The first ref is kept in the session and saved on the account at sign-up (`signup_ref`), and counted under Admin > Sign-up sources. No trackers and no cookies beyond the session (`README.md#sign-up-sources`).
- **Company logos:** pages only show logos stored on Nextward. Outside logo URLs are downloaded by `companies:logos` and never sent to the browser (`README.md#local-setup`).
- **Design:** the whole app follows the Hired 128 Plus Webflow template (https://hired-128-plus.webflow.io/), owner, 26 October 2026: Poppins everywhere (no other font), cobalt `#0047FF` for actions, navy `#001F6D` for headings and dark bands, ice `#F2F6FF` for light bands, 10px corners and uppercase buttons with wide letter spacing. Text is light: weights 400 and 500, buttons 400. In the workspace, cards are white with no border on the ice page, fields are filled ice with no border (white when they sit on the page itself), and the dashboard opens with a navy band like the public hero. We keep our own rules over the template: flat surfaces, no shadows or glows, and no one-sided accent borders (top stripes, left bars); use tints or solid fills instead. This holds for emails too. The public pages, sign-in pages, emails and the whole workspace use it now. Details in `design/DESIGN.md`.
- **Styling:** Tailwind first. Style components with utility classes in the template, and write plain CSS only for what Tailwind can't do well (HTML from Markdown, keyframes, `:has()` layout rules). Styles used by one component stay in that component; when a component needs a lot of plain CSS, put it in a file with the same name next to it (like `Pages/Blog/Show.css`). `resources/css/` keeps only what several components share: tokens in `app.css`, `elements.css`, `public.css`, `workspace.css` and `fonts.css`, plus `cv.css` for PDFs. Use px breakpoints (`max-[768px]:`) and don't mix them with named ones (`max-lg:`) on one element, because Tailwind puts the named ones last. Details in `design/INTEGRATION.md`.
- **One home page:** the home page speaks to students and professionals alike; there are no separate audience pages (old links redirect home). About explains the mission, what Nextward covers, where listings come from and who builds it. It says the site is free to use and the AI tools use credits (20 free, then 5 a month), without detailing prices.
- **Signed-in pages live under `/app`:** dashboard, profile, settings, applications, messages, CV & letters, saved roles and searches, credits, the workspace Talent and Companies lists, posting and editing roles, and every admin page (`/app/admin/...`). Only `/app` pages have the sidebar and workspace header. Opportunities have an in-app copy (`/app/opportunities`, `/app/opportunities/{id}-{slug}`), and inside `/app` listing and company links stay in `/app`. Public pages (`/`, `/opportunities`, `/companies`, `/candidates`, the blog...) always use the public design, signed in or not; their header links to `/app/dashboard` and the sidebar links back to the website. `/app` is `noindex` and blocked in `robots.txt`. Old links (`/dashboard`, `/applications/5`, `/admin/imports`...) redirect to `/app`. Sign-in, sign-up, password and email verification pages stay where they are.
- **Public pages:** one design for every public page: a navy header that turns cobalt on scroll (links Roles, Companies, Talent, Blog and About, and one "Get started" button to sign up, or "Dashboard" when signed in), a navy hero with thin curved lines (`LandingHero`, centred), white and ice blue (`#F2F6FF`) sections with centred titles, a navy key features band, and an ice footer ("We would love to help", links, copyright). The closing card is a rounded cobalt card that sits half over the footer. No cream, beige or ivory. Every public page uses it, the legal pages included. Details in `LANDING_PAGE_DESIGN.md`.
- **Companies directory:** visitors can search companies at `/companies` (and `/fr/companies`) and open each one's roles; the home page shows the companies hiring now. It lists hiring companies by default and never shows merged or nameless companies. Signed-in users have the workspace list at `/app/companies`, which uses the same company cards. Each card shows the company's short description (or "No description yet." in light grey), its place, type and size, and how many roles it has open; cards don't list the roles themselves. Company pages (`/companies/{id}-{name}` and `/app/companies/{id}`) follow the talent profile layout: a navy banner with the logo, Verified badge and quick facts, then About and the open roles, and a side column with hiring numbers (open roles, jobs, internships, work modes, fields, countries and the skills its roles ask for) and company facts (head office, founded, size, type, website, careers page, LinkedIn, and when the website was checked).
- **Public images:** cartoon mascots and the desk scene from `public/nextward-assets/mascots/`, used wherever the template has photos; generated photos from `landing-light-v1/` only as article images. Never present the photos as real users, staff, offices or partners.
- **Writing:** plain, everyday words in copy, docs, commits and code comments. Comments are short and rare.
- **Email:** sent through Resend from `nextward@alabaganne.com` until a Nextward domain is verified. Every email uses the shared layout in `resources/views/vendor/mail` (new logo, white card with no borders, solid blue card for quoted text, uppercase cobalt button). Text people type is shown as plain text. Details in `README.md#email`.
- **Contact and feedback:** one form at `/contact` for help, bug reports, feature ideas, listing problems, and hiring or partnerships (`?topic=bug` and the like pick a topic). Messages are emailed to `MAIL_CONTACT_ADDRESS` and not stored. Bug reports add the browser; help and bug messages can carry one screenshot and the page the person came from.
- **Email verification:** required before applying, messaging, posting or editing roles, saving searches with alerts, claiming a company and asking for a public profile. Browsing and the dashboard work without it.
- **Changing email:** in Settings > Security, with the current password. A verified email changes only after a link sent to the old address is opened; then the new address is verified as usual. An unverified email changes right away. The profile form never changes the email.
- **Public candidate profiles:** private by default. A candidate asks from their profile page (`/app/profile`), an admin approves under Candidates, and only then the profile shows at `/candidates`. Its link is `/candidates/{username}` once the candidate picks a username in Settings (3 to 30 lowercase letters, numbers or dashes, starting with a letter; route words like `manage` are reserved), otherwise `/candidates/{id}-{name}`; the old link redirects to the current one. The username is not asked at sign-up, and only candidates have one. It shows name, headline, field, location, years of experience, bio, skills, languages, work history, education, projects, website and LinkedIn. Resume, email and phone show to every visitor only when the candidate ticks a separate box on their profile page (off by default, `public_contact`). The directory is linked as "Talent" in the public navbar so companies can find people, and can be searched by name, role, skill or location and filtered by field or shared resume. Admins and companies also get a Talent page in the workspace (`/app/talent`) with the same profiles, cards and search; its cards open the profile inside the workspace at `/app/talent/{username or id-name}`. These pages are `noindex` and stay out of the sitemap. The candidate can go private at any time; editing an approved profile keeps it live.
- **Demo accounts:** production has two demo accounts for trying the site, `company@example.com` (Demo Company, with four published listings and a draft) and `student@example.com` (a candidate who applied to three of them), both with the password `password`, made by `DemoAccountsSeeder` (owner, 26 October 2026). Their listings say they are fictional. Run it with `--class`; plain `php artisan db:seed` doesn't include it.
- **Admin accounts** are candidates too: each admin has a candidate profile (made by `nextward:make-admin`, or the first time they open Settings or apply), so they fill in the same profile and resume as anyone else. Admins can apply on Nextward or track an outside application like any candidate; a listing page shows them the apply options first and a Manage listing box (edit, review applicants, remove) below. They can't move their own application along.
- **Outside applications:** on listings that are applied to on the employer's site, the Apply button on the listing page and on the CV & letter page marks the listing opened as soon as it is clicked (and the matching job lead Opened). An "I applied" button next to it saves the application to the candidate's private tracker in one click and stays on the page; notes, date and status are edited on the tracker page.
- **Sources abroad:** Nextward no longer reads Tunisian job sites (Tanitjobs, Keejob or any `.tn` site); imports come from employer boards and career sites abroad, and source discovery turns Tunisian sites down. Their listings were closed and stay archived. Employer boards on Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, Workable and Recruitee publish as usual. Y Combinator startup jobs come from the companies' own boards (Stripe, Docker, Vanta, Clipboard Health, Photoroom and others in `ImportSourceSeeder`), never from ycombinator.com/jobs or Work at a Startup, whose terms forbid scraping (owner, 27 October 2026). Remote job boards (Himalayas, Remote OK, Remotive, We Work Remotely and the Hacker News "Who is hiring" thread) are read only for private job leads (`publication_mode` `leads`, state `lead`) and never published, because their terms don't allow passing their jobs on; a board job that links to an employer ATS adds that board as a pending source to approve. Source discovery looks for jobs, not internships: employers that hire fully remote people in Tunisia, or offer visa sponsorship or relocation in Europe and the USA, above all Germany, the USA, France, Italy, Spain, Poland, Belgium and Romania.
- **Profiles decide what is added:** imports only add a listing that a profile wants. Nextward is the owner's own job search for now (owner, 25 October 2026), so only admins' candidate profiles count (`importer.profiles.from`; `OPPORTUNITY_PROFILES_FROM=everyone` counts every signed-up candidate again), plus profiles the AI reads from resumes with `opportunities:profile` (`search_profiles`). A candidate profile is the field, preferred fields, skills, kinds, levels and countries from Settings > Job preferences, and its "I need a visa to work abroad" box (`needs_visa`). A profile wants a listing when it takes its kind, level and place and shares its field or two skills. The rest wait as `unmatched` and are checked again each day. `example.com` demo accounts don't count; with no profiles every IT listing is added. A profile with `needs_visa` (someone living in Tunisia, like the owner) only wants on-site or hybrid roles that offer visa sponsorship or relocation, in its preferred countries, and remote roles open to people in Tunisia (`App\Leads\Eligibility`). The owner's own focus: jobs, not internships for now; software engineering, web, mobile, general software development and data science and AI; in Europe and the USA, above all Germany, the USA, France, Italy, Spain, Poland, Belgium and Romania; a visa is needed for on-site and hybrid roles there. The owner's profile is set that way (jobs only, those countries plus the rest of Europe, needs a visa), so imports add almost no internships. `opportunities:fetch` only adds listings posted in the last 14 days.
- **Job leads:** an admin-only page (`/app/leads`, sidebar "Job leads") with the owner's daily list of software roles they can take from Tunisia. It is stored per candidate (`leads`, `lead_contacts`, `lead_skips`) so it can open to all candidates later. `leads:daily` runs at 07:00 Tunis time: listings first seen in the last 3 days and private job board finds, scored frontend first, then full stack, applied AI and backend; mid to senior only (no junior, intern, staff, principal or 8+ years), at most 12 a day. A job never shows twice (one lead per job key, close titles at the same company count as the same job), reposts are left out, and after applying at a company its other roles stay hidden for 60 days and show under "Hidden today" with an "Already applied" badge. Every job left out is logged with its reason under "Left out". Statuses only move forward on their own (To apply, Opened, Applied, Interviewing, Offer, Rejected, Skipped); opening a posting marks it Opened, and applying or tracking on Nextward marks it Applied. Applications made elsewhere come in from jobs.json, Tsenta or LinkedIn exports, any CSV or JSON, and the Add a job form. Each company can have contacts to reach out to, with a Contacted tick; "Suggest a contact" asks the AI to search public pages and nothing is saved until the owner keeps it. Gmail can be connected read only (`gmail.readonly`, owner only): confirmation and rejection emails become leads, and only their subject, sender and first lines may go to the AI. Nothing reads, posts to or automates LinkedIn.
- **Verified companies:** a new imported listing is only added once its company's own website is found and checked (`importer.verify_companies`). The AI searches the web for the website and public facts (`App\Ai\Agents\CompanyResearcher`); `App\Importing\CompanyVerifier` then opens the site (social networks, job boards and directories don't count) and it must show the company name or link to its job board. A passed company gets `verified_at`, a Verified badge on its cards and pages, and fills only empty fields: website, about, head office, size, founding year, startup or established, LinkedIn page, careers page and icon. Listings wait as `waiting` ("Company website not checked yet.") and are rejected after two failed tries a day apart ("Company website not found."); admins can still publish them by hand. Companies with an account, job leads and listings already online are not held back, and companies that can't be verified keep their listings without the badge. A company whose website already belongs to another company is merged into it and its name becomes an alias. The badge is a solid blue seal with a white tick (a white seal on navy banners): the seal alone on cards, the seal and the word Verified on pages.
- **One job, one listing:** listings are matched by company and title, not only by link. Company names lose legal suffixes (Inc, Ltd, GmbH) and follow `company_aliases` ("Tether Operations Limited" is Tether); titles lose seniority words, work modes and place suffixes (`App\Importing\JobKey`). The same job on two boards, or a closed job posted again with a new id, stays one listing within 90 days; a second open opening with the same title on the same board stays separate, unless both are the same posting link once tracking is taken off (`url_key`) with the same place. Every role of the company in those 90 days is checked, and new listings are added one at a time (a cache lock) so two runs can't add the same job at once. `first_posted_at` keeps the first date the job was posted: an employer ATS date beats a board date, and a later date marks `reposted_at` instead of a new job.
- **Error monitoring:** none for now (Laravel logs only), by the owner's choice.
- **What is free:** browsing, saving, applying, alerts, tracking, resumes made from the profile, themes and downloads are free for everyone. AI calls cost credits: one credit per kit (tailored CV and cover letter for one role), resume translation or editable copy of an uploaded PDF, each with a few rewrites, and one per resume read. Credits are on: 20 at sign-up and 5 more on the 1st of each month for candidates with a verified email (`credits:monthly`). Failed AI calls give the credit back; admins never pay. Never block a free feature behind credits.
- **Beta credits:** while Nextward is in beta (`BILLING_BETA=true`, the default), payments are locked: the Credits page shows the packs and payment methods greyed out under a lock ("Payments are coming soon", "Price coming soon" for packs with no price), and checkout is refused. Candidates with a verified email can get 100 free credits (`BILLING_BETA_CREDITS`) as often as they like, from the credits panel in the top bar or the Credits page; there is no limit while the beta lasts (the route is only rate-limited). Turn it off when payments open.
- **Credits in the top bar:** candidates see their balance in the workspace top bar, not the sidebar. Hovering it (or tapping it) opens a panel with the balance, what a credit pays for, the next monthly credits, the beta note and the free credits button, and a "Buy more credits" link to the Credits page.
- **Payments:** Konnect and Flouci for Tunisian payers (TND), Lemon Squeezy for international payers (USD), for packs of 20, 60 and 150 credits. Outside the beta, packs stay hidden until the owner sets their prices. Payments are always checked with the provider before credits are added. Details in `README.md#credits`.
- **Resumes:** a candidate keeps up to 10 resumes on their profile page: uploaded PDFs, resumes made from their profile, AI translations into English or French, and editable copies of uploaded PDFs, which the AI copies word for word in their own language. Translations and copies are drafts the candidate checks and saves. One is the main resume: shared on the public profile, read to fill the profile, given to the AI for kits and picked first when applying. When applying they choose which resume or tailored CV to send; a copy is stored with the application.
- **Resume themes:** resumes and kit CVs use the six Resume Studio themes (`app/Support/CvTemplates.php`), printed to PDF by headless Chrome and also downloadable as Word. They are the candidate's own documents, so they may keep their accent rules and colour bands; the no-stripe rule is for Nextward's own pages and emails.
- **Kit language:** kits are written in the listing's own language when a CV can be written in it (English, French, Arabic, German, Spanish, Italian, Portuguese, Dutch), with English and French offered too. The original listing text goes into the prompt. Arabic CVs and letters are right to left in every theme, with self-hosted Arabic fonts. AI resume translations stay English and French.
- **AI output:** the AI only uses facts from the candidate's own profile and resume, and people always review and edit before anything is saved or sent.

## Stack

Laravel 13 (PHP 8.3+), Inertia 3 with SSR, Vue 3, Tailwind CSS 4, Vite 8, Node 24, and headless Chrome (Browsershot) for resume and letter PDFs. Run `php artisan test` and `npm run build` before you commit.
