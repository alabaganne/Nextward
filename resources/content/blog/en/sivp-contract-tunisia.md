The SIVP (stage d'initiation à la vie professionnelle) is the old name of the CIVP, the contrat d'initiation à la vie professionnelle run by ANETI, Tunisia's national employment agency. It lets a private company host a first-time job seeker for 12 months. For a higher-education graduate, ANETI pays 200 DT a month and the company must add at least 200 DT a month.

## Is the SIVP the same as the CIVP?

Yes. Government decree no. 2019-542 of 28 May 2019 reorganized the programs of the National Employment Fund (Fonds national de l'emploi). The SIVP became the CIVP, and the state allowance for graduates rose from 150 DT to 200 DT.

Many people and job ads still say « SIVP ». ANETI's website uses « CIVP ». Some older pages online still show the old 150 DT amount for graduates, so always check the date of what you read.

## Who can get an SIVP (CIVP) contract?

According to ANETI, the CIVP is open to people who are:

- Tunisian nationals,
- first-time job seekers (primo-chercheurs d'emploi),
- holders of a higher-education diploma, an equivalent diploma, or a certified BTS.

### What « first-time job seeker » means

ANETI counts you as a first-time job seeker if, since your last diploma, you have not worked in an economic activity for:

- more than 24 months in a row, or
- more than 36 months in total, with breaks.

### Who is exempt from this condition

Two groups do not need to be first-time job seekers: job seekers without a higher-education diploma or BTS, and people with disabilities.

### Which employers can host you

All private-sector companies and liberal professions (for example, a doctor's practice, a law firm or an accounting firm). ANETI's page does not mention an age limit. If your situation is unusual, ask your local employment office, the BETI (Bureau de l'emploi et du travail indépendant).

## How long does an SIVP last?

The contract lasts 12 months.

ANETI's page says that, as an exception, it may extend the contract by up to 12 more months with the same company, or approve a second contract with another company. This applies to higher-education graduates and BTS holders. The total can never go above 24 months.

In September 2025, ANETI's director general announced that from 1 October 2025, CIVP contracts would no longer be extended within the same company. He said a job seeker can still reach 24 months in total, but only across different companies. Ask your BETI which rule applies to you before you count on a second year.

## How much does an SIVP pay?

The CIVP comes with a monthly allowance (indemnité). Part is paid by ANETI and part by the company. Here are the amounts on ANETI's page:

| Monthly amount | Higher-education diploma or BTS | Other levels |
|---|---|---|
| Paid by ANETI | 200 DT | 150 DT |
| Paid by the company (minimum) | 200 DT | 150 DT |
| Minimum total | 400 DT | 300 DT |

People with disabilities receive an extra 50 DT a month from ANETI, whatever their level of study.

The company amount is a minimum. A company may pay more, so ask for the exact figure and check that it is written in your contract.

## What else does the CIVP cover?

ANETI's page lists three more benefits:

- **Social coverage** for the whole contract.
- **Extra training** organized during the contract, up to 400 hours.
- **A hiring incentive for the company.** If the company hires a higher-education graduate from its CIVP interns on a permanent contract (CDI), the state covers the employer's social security contribution on that salary for two years, up to 600 DT a month.

### A rule that protects you

A company can take new CIVP interns only if it has hired at least 50% of the CIVP interns who finished with it during the three years before its new request. If it has not, it must wait at least one year after its last contract ends.

This rule matters to you. Before you sign, ask the company how many of its past CIVP interns it hired.

## How do you apply for an SIVP?

The company starts the request, but you need to be ready.

1. **Register as a job seeker with ANETI.** You can do it at your BETI or through the online registration on ANETI's website.
2. **Find a company that wants to host you.** Browse [graduate programs](/opportunities?program_type=graduate) and [entry-level jobs](/opportunities/jobs?seniority=entry), and ask employers whether they hire through the CIVP.
3. **The company creates the CIVP request** on ANETI's platform and enters your details.
4. **You confirm it.** You get a notification, log in to your personal space and confirm the request.
5. **Your BETI processes the file.** The online process is still being rolled out across employment offices, so your office may also ask for paper documents.

### Documents to prepare

ANETI's page lists these documents for the file:

- 4 copies of the CIVP contract,
- 2 copies of your national ID card (CIN),
- 1 certified copy of your diploma or its equivalence,
- 2 bank or postal account details (RIB or RIP) with an official stamp,
- a CNSS non-affiliation certificate, or your CNSS history if you have already worked,
- a valid disability card, if it applies to you.

The company adds its CNSS affiliation certificate and its tax ID card the first time it deals with the BETI.

## What should you check before you sign?

- **The total monthly amount**, and when the company pays its part.
- **Your tasks and your supervisor.** A CIVP should teach you a job, not just fill a gap.
- **The start date**, and whether the file is complete at the BETI.
- **The company's record** of hiring past CIVP interns.
- **What happens after 12 months.** Ask openly whether a work contract is possible.

## How do the PFE, the SIVP and a first job fit together?

A PFE is an internship you do as a student, under your school's agreement. A CIVP comes after graduation. Many graduates go from a [PFE internship](/blog/pfe-internship-tunisia) to a CIVP, then to a work contract. Our guide to your [first job after graduation](/blog/first-job-after-graduation-tunisia) covers the other paths. Keep your [CV](/blog/internship-cv) up to date at each step.
