## Public, searchable internship listings

A good internship platform should make opportunities discoverable through search engines and easy browsing. Students should be able to see real internship details before creating an account.

## Student-friendly applications

Students need a simple way to apply, attach documents, save opportunities, and follow their application status. The experience should feel calm and organized.

## Company dashboards that stay focused

Companies need tools to publish internships, review applications, manage candidates, and communicate clearly. Too many unrelated features make the workflow slower.

## Trust and profile context

Company profiles, student skills, internship descriptions, and communication history all help both sides make better decisions with less uncertainty.
