## Read the applicant location requirement

A remote role may still limit applicants to a country or region. Check the actual hiring requirements, including any exclusions, instead of assuming that a remote label allows work from anywhere. A company operating globally does not, by itself, establish eligibility for a particular role.

## Check the practical working arrangement

Look for required working hours, travel, office visits, and the stated employment arrangement. A time-zone preference and a country eligibility rule answer different questions. If the listing does not say whether Tunisia is eligible, ask the employer before investing in a long application.

## Treat relocation and visa sponsorship separately

Relocation assistance may describe travel or moving support. Visa sponsorship is a separate detail. Record only what the employer confirms, and use the official application page to check the current requirements. Nextward displays an unknown state when the source does not establish an answer.
