## Write internship posts with specific outcomes

Students apply more confidently when the role explains what they will learn, what they will build or support, and what skills are required. Avoid vague descriptions and list the actual responsibilities.

## Reduce application friction

Long forms discourage strong candidates. Ask for the essentials first: profile, resume or attachment, relevant skills, and a short motivation. Extra screening can happen later.

## Centralize review and communication

A dedicated internship platform helps companies publish roles, receive applications, review students, and message candidates without losing context across email threads and spreadsheets.

## Move quickly with promising students

Early-career candidates often apply to many roles at once. Fast responses, clear next steps, and respectful communication help your company stand out.
