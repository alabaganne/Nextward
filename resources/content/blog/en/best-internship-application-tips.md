## Make the first scan easy

Recruiters and company teams often review applications quickly. Use a clean resume, clear section titles, simple dates, and direct project descriptions. If your strongest skills are hidden, they may be missed.

## Match the role instead of sending the same text

Read the internship description and adjust your application to match the requirements. Mention relevant coursework, projects, tools, or experience that connect directly to the position.

## Show proof of ability

For technical, design, writing, or marketing internships, proof matters. Link to projects, portfolios, case studies, GitHub repositories, campaigns, or examples of work that show what you can actually do.

## Follow up professionally

If the platform allows messaging or the company provides contact details, send a short polite follow-up after a reasonable time. Keep it concise and focused on your interest in the role.
