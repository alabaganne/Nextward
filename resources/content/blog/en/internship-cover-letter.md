A PFE cover letter should fit well under one page, about 250 to 350 words, and answer three questions: which PFE you want and when, why this company and this topic, and what proof you bring. End with your dates and a clear request for an interview. The same structure works for the email you send with your CV.

## Do you still need a cover letter for a PFE internship?

Often, yes. Many PFE offers and company PFE books ask for a CV and a cover letter. Even when they do not, the email you send is read like a letter.

A short, specific letter helps a recruiter choose between two similar CVs. It also shows that you read the topic.

## How long should a PFE cover letter be?

Keep it to three or four short paragraphs. If you write it directly in the email body, make it even shorter: around 120 to 180 words.

Recruiters read many applications for one topic. A letter they can read in one minute has a better chance than a full page of general sentences.

## What structure works for a PFE cover letter?

1. **Header.** Your name and contact details, the date, the company and, if you know it, the person's name.
2. **Subject line.** « Application for the PFE internship [topic or reference] – from [month and year] ».
3. **Opening.** Who you are and which PFE you want.
4. **Why them.** One specific reason linked to the topic, a product or a technology.
5. **What you bring.** One or two pieces of proof, with results.
6. **Practical details.** Your start date, the length your school requires, where you can work, and the internship agreement.
7. **Closing.** Ask for an interview and thank the reader.

### Get to the point in the opening

Skip « I am writing to you in order to apply ». Start with the facts: your year, your specialty, your school and the topic you want.

### Give one real reason for choosing them

Generic praise like « a leader in its field » tells the recruiter nothing. Name the topic, a product you used, a tool in the offer, or a project the company has published.

### Bring proof, not adjectives

« Motivated, dynamic and serious » appears in most letters. Replace it with a project, what you did, and what came out of it. The proof shows those qualities without naming them.

## What does a good PFE cover letter look like?

Here is an example for a data topic from a company's PFE book. Replace everything in brackets, and change the projects to your own.

> **Subject: Application for the PFE internship « Real-time fraud detection » (ref. [reference])**
>
> Dear Ms [Last name],
>
> I am a final-year engineering student in data science at [school], and I am applying for your PFE topic « Real-time fraud detection », listed in your PFE book. I am available from [start date] for the [length] my school requires.
>
> This topic matches the work I enjoy most. For my PFA, I built a model that flags unusual card payments on a public dataset. By testing three methods and tuning the best one, I raised the share of fraud cases caught from 71% to 86%. The code and report are on my GitHub.
>
> During my summer internship at [company], I wrote Python scripts that cut a weekly report from one day of work to two hours. I also used Kafka in a class project, a tool your topic lists for real-time data.
>
> I would be glad to discuss the topic with your team, at your office in [city] or online. My school will send the internship agreement as soon as you confirm.
>
> Thank you for your time.
>
> [First name Last name]
> [Phone] · [Email] · [GitHub link]

### Why this example works

- The topic and dates are in the first paragraph.
- Each claim comes with a result or a link.
- It names a tool from the offer and shows the student has used it.
- It ends with a clear next step.

## How do you write the application email?

When you apply by email, the email is the first thing the recruiter reads.

- **Subject line:** reuse the offer's reference, or write « PFE application – [specialty] – [First name Last name] – [start month] ».
- **Body:** four to six lines. Who you are, which topic, one piece of proof, what is attached, thanks.
- **Attachments:** your CV and letter as PDFs, named like « CV-Firstname-Lastname-PFE.pdf ».
- **Sender:** an email address built on your name.

Example body:

> Dear Ms [Last name],
>
> I am a final-year data science student at [school] and I am applying for your PFE topic « Real-time fraud detection » (ref. [reference]). My PFA was a fraud detection model that caught 86% of fraud cases on a public dataset. You will find my CV and cover letter attached.
>
> I am available from [start date]. Thank you for your time.
>
> [First name Last name] – [Phone]

## What mistakes get a cover letter ignored?

- The wrong company name, left over from another letter.
- Repeating the CV line by line.
- Talking only about what you want to learn, and never about what you bring.
- Empty phrases like « dynamic and motivated ».
- No start date or length.
- Typos in the company's name or the topic title.
- A letter that runs past one page.

## How do you adapt the letter to other cases?

### For a summer internship

Make it shorter. Say what type of internship your school requires, your dates, and what you want to learn. Reliability and curiosity matter more than technical proof. Our [summer internship guide](/blog/summer-internship-tunisia) covers the timing.

### For a spontaneous application

Name the team you want to join and suggest one or two topic ideas linked to the company's work. It shows initiative and makes the reply easier.

### For a topic from a PFE book

Quote the topic title and reference exactly. Some companies post dozens of topics, and the reference helps them sort applications.
