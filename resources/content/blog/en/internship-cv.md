A good internship CV fits on one page, says at the top which internship you want, and proves your skills with projects, courses and activities instead of job titles. Write it in the language of the offer, adapt the top third to each role, and send it as a PDF with a clear file name.

## What should an internship CV include?

Keep the sections in this order. Recruiters read from the top and decide fast.

1. **Header.** Full name, phone, a simple email address, your city, and links (LinkedIn, GitHub or portfolio).
2. **Target line.** One line on the internship you want, for example: « PFE internship in data engineering – available from February 2027 ».
3. **Education.** Degree, school, current year and expected graduation date. Add two or three courses if they match the offer.
4. **Projects.** Class projects, PFA, personal projects, hackathons. For most students, this is the strongest section.
5. **Experience.** Summer internships, part-time jobs, freelance work, tutoring.
6. **Skills.** Tools and technologies, grouped by type. For example: « Languages: Python, SQL. Tools: Git, Power BI. »
7. **Languages.** With an honest level: native, fluent, professional, or a CEFR level like B2.
8. **Activities.** Clubs, volunteering and sport, with your role.

## How long should an internship CV be?

One page. Recruiters often screen many applications for a single internship, so they read quickly.

If your CV runs onto a second page, cut in this order:

- high-school details beyond your baccalauréat,
- activities that lasted only a few days,
- skills you could not explain in an interview,
- long sentences that can become short bullet points.

## How do you fill an internship CV with no experience?

Projects and activities count, as long as you describe them like real work.

### Describe projects like jobs

For each project, give the title, the context (course, PFA, personal, team size), what you did, the tools, and the result.

- **Weak:** « Website project for a course. »
- **Strong:** « Built a booking web app in a team of 3 (Laravel, MySQL) for a course project. Wrote the payment module and its tests. Code and demo on GitHub. »

The strong version tells the recruiter what you can do on day one.

### Use action verbs and real numbers

Start each bullet with a verb: built, analyzed, designed, led, organized, tested, reduced. Add numbers when they are true: users, records, members, hours saved, budget. One honest number beats three vague adjectives.

### Count clubs and volunteering

Treasurer of a student club, organizer of a 200-person event, tutor for younger students: these show responsibility and teamwork. Give your role and one result, not just the club's name.

### Add online courses with care

Online courses and certificates show that you learn on your own. List only the ones linked to the offer, with the platform and the year, for example « SQL for data analysis – online course, 2026 ». Three relevant courses say more than a list of fifteen. If you built something during a course, put it under Projects instead, where it gets more attention.

## Should your CV be in French or English?

Match the offer. If the offer is in French, send a French CV. If it is in English, send an English one.

In Tunisia, many local companies work mainly in French, while international companies, remote roles and many IT teams use English. Keep both versions ready, with the same content, and never mix two languages in one CV.

## Which personal details should you include?

- **Include:** name, phone, email, city and your links.
- **Optional:** a photo. It is common on Tunisian CVs but not required. If you add one, use a recent, simple photo with a plain background.
- **Leave out:** your national ID (CIN) number and your full home address. Your city is enough. Marital status and date of birth are not needed for most internships.

Use an email address built on your name. Create a new one if your current address is a nickname.

## How do you adapt your CV to each offer?

Adapting takes ten minutes and makes a real difference.

1. Read the offer and mark the skills and tools it names.
2. Rewrite your target line to match the role.
3. Move the most relevant project to the top of the Projects section.
4. Use the offer's own words when they are true for you, such as « Power BI » rather than « a dashboard tool ».
5. Remove lines that do not help this application.

Some companies use software to sort CVs before a person reads them. A simple one-column layout with real text, not an image, is easier for both the software and the recruiter.

## What mistakes should you avoid on an internship CV?

- Typos, or the wrong company name in your target line.
- A nickname email address.
- Long paragraphs instead of short bullet points.
- Skill bars or stars (« Python ★★★★☆ ») that say nothing concrete.
- A Word file whose layout breaks on another computer.
- Skills you cannot explain. Interviewers will ask about every line.

## What should you check before you send your CV?

| Check | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| One page, saved as PDF | Easy to read, and the layout does not move |
| File name like « CV-Firstname-Lastname-PFE.pdf » | Easy to find in a busy inbox |
| Target line matches the offer | Shows you read it |
| Every link opens | Recruiters do click them |
| No typos | The first sign of care |
| Read aloud once | Catches odd or missing words |
