# Public pages design — Nextward

> Updated 27 October 2026. The whole app follows the Hired 128 Plus Webflow template (https://hired-128-plus.webflow.io/). Every public page, the sign-in pages, the emails, the alert unsubscribe page and the signed-in workspace use it (see `TODOS.md` for what is left).

## Direction

A bright, friendly site about the next step in a career, for students and professionals, covering IT internships and jobs around the world. Keep the existing Nextward logo (a white version is coming).

- **Font:** Poppins 400, 500 and 600, self-hosted. Hero titles 500 at `clamp(40px, 5.6vw, 72px)`, line height 1.1. Section titles 400 at `clamp(30px, 3.4vw, 46px)`, line height 1.2. Card titles 19–22px 500. Body 16–18px, line height 1.7.
- **Colours:** cobalt `#0047FF` (`brand`) for actions and links, navy `#001F6D` (`navy`) for headings, the hero and dark bands, ice `#F2F6FF` (`pale`) for light bands and the footer, body text `#656D80` (`subtle`), hairlines `#E6E9F0` (`line`). One word or phrase in a heading can be cobalt (`.accent`); on navy it is the lighter `#2F6BFF`. No cream, beige or ivory.
- **Labels:** small uppercase text with wide letter spacing (`.landing-label`, 15px, `tracking-caps` .1em) above section titles.
- **Buttons** (`.cta`): uppercase, weight 400, letter spacing .1em, 10px corners, 1px border. `cta-primary` (cobalt fill, turns clear with navy text on hover), `cta-secondary` (white with a cobalt border, fills cobalt on hover), `cta-light` (white on dark), `cta-ghost` (white outline on dark).
- **Header:** navy at the top of the page, solid cobalt once the page scrolls. White links with wide spacing (Roles, Companies, Talent, Blog, About), the language menu and one white button: "Get started" ("Commencer"), which opens sign-up, or "Dashboard" ("Mon espace") when signed in. The sign-up page links to log in at the top.
- **Words:** visible copy says "role" and "roles" ("offre", "offres") for listings, never "opportunity". URLs and code keep `opportunities`.
- **Hero** (`LandingHero`): navy with thin curved white lines (`resources/images/hero-lines.svg`, our own drawing, through `.line-art`), centred title, label, lead and buttons. With `overlap` it leaves room for a white card the page pulls up over its bottom edge (`.hero-overlap`). Mascots can stand at its bottom corners on wide screens.
- **Sections:** white and ice bands, centred title blocks (`.section-intro`, max 740px) above grids, about 88px of padding. A navy band with line art for the key features grid, with 50px icon tiles.
- **Cards:** 10px corners (`rounded-card`), a 1px `line` border or an ice fill. No shadows, glows, gradients or one-sided accent stripes; the template's soft shadows become borders or tints.
- **FAQ:** native `<details>` split by hairlines, with a round 40px toggle that turns cobalt when open.
- **Closing card:** a rounded cobalt card with line art, centred title and buttons, half over the page and half over the ice footer.
- **Footer:** ice. "We would love to help" with contact links, the logo with the page links, a hairline, then the centred copyright. On pages without the closing card a hairline sits above it, so an ice page body and the footer stay apart.
- **Images:** cartoon mascots wherever the template uses photos; the night desk scene (`21-cozy-career-workspace-night.webp`) fills the navy employer card on the home page, with white text on its empty left side; article photos only on guide cards and article pages.
- **Layout:** 1200px wide, 20px side padding on phones.

Pages are styled with Tailwind classes; classes shared by several pages are in `resources/css/public.css`. The hero, the closing card and the art panel (a mascot on an ice panel with a cobalt or navy block set off behind it, used for split sections) are shared components in `resources/js/Components/Landing/`.

## Pages

Keep each landing page short. Don't add a second block that explains the same thing.

- **Home** (one page for everyone; `/for-students` and `/for-professionals` redirect here with a 301): centred navy hero "Find your next opportunity." with two mascots and two buttons, then the search card over the hero edge (keyword and type) with popular searches. Then the companies hiring now (up to eight logos with their open-role counts, linking to `/companies`), recent opportunities on ice, "How it works" as three numbered cobalt circles joined by a dashed line, the navy key features grid, the AI CV and cover letter split with a mascot, the navy employer card with the night desk scene, the FAQ (text in `config/nextward_public.php`, also sent as `FAQPage` data), three guide cards and the closing card.
- **Opportunities:** a centred navy hero with the page title, then one white card over the hero edge with the tabs All / Internships / Jobs and the open and archived counts, the search box, and one row of quick filters (field, skills, type, country, work mode, experience, date) plus an "Open worldwide" toggle, all applied as soon as they change. Field and Skills open a panel inside the card. Field lets people pick several fields at once; when more than one is picked, each shows as a chip you can remove. There is no "More filters" panel. Filters with no control here (contract, city, employer, pay, programme, salary, listing language, duration, start date from older links and saved searches) still apply and show as chips you can remove. Results sit on ice in two columns when there is room. In `/app` the same card shows under a plain title.
- **Opportunity page:** a navy header with the breadcrumb, logo, kind label, title, company and field, and the facts as small tags ("Open worldwide" in green). Below, on ice: white cards for the description and skills, the kit panel, location and who can apply, and source and dates; on the side, the apply card (pay first, then the apply buttons, save and copy link, and the admin tools), the company card and the report box; then more opportunities. In `/app` the header is a white card.
- **Companies** (`/companies`, `/fr/companies`): a centred navy hero "Companies hiring in IT", then one white card over its edge with the company search (name or city) and chips for "Hiring now" (the default), each IT field with open roles, and "All companies". Company cards sit on ice in up to three columns: logo, name, place, type and size, the short description and the open-role count. A closing card "Hiring in IT?" links to the company sign-up (or to posting a role for company accounts). Signed-in users see this public page too; their workspace companies list is at `/app/companies`.
- **Company page:** a navy header with the breadcrumb and the profile banner (logo, Verified badge, quick facts and buttons: open roles in white, website and LinkedIn as outlines), then on ice the About and open roles cards, and on the side the hiring numbers and company facts.
- **Talent** (`/candidates`, `/fr/candidates`): the same pattern as companies: a centred navy hero, a white card over its edge with the search and the field and "Resume shared" chips, candidate cards on ice, numbered pages and the "Want your profile here?" closing card. A candidate page has the navy header with the profile banner (resume in white, portfolio and LinkedIn as outlines), then About, experience, education and projects on ice, with contact and skills on the side. In `/app/talent` the banner is a navy card instead.
- **Career guides** (`/blog`, `/fr/blog`): a centred navy hero “A little guidance for your next move.”, then one white card over its edge with the search box and category chips that filter the posts on the page (with a reset and an empty state). Below, on white, the newest guide as the featured one (photo on the left, “Latest guide” label, title, excerpt and date), then the other guides in three columns of guide cards and the closing card.
- **Article page:** a centred navy header with the back link and the other-language link, the category tag, date and read time, the title and the excerpt. The photo sits over the header edge (up to 880px wide, since the built-in photos are small). Then the readable body with the contents list on the side, the FAQ, matching role chips on ice and a byline with the author's initials. Related guides follow under a hairline (“More guides”, three cards and a View all button), then the closing card.
- **About** (`/about`, `/fr/about`): a centred navy hero “IT careers, made clearer.” with two buttons. Then the mission split (text and where listings come from, with the student and company mascots on an art panel), “One place for IT careers” on ice with three white cards (every IT role, both languages, help to apply), “Free to use, with AI help when you apply” (`#ai-tools`, an art panel on the left, a checklist of the AI tools, one line on what is free and no pricing details), the founder in a navy card with line art and contact links, and the closing card.
- **Contact** (`/contact`): a centred navy hero “How can we help?” and, over its edge on ice, the form in a white card next to a side column with a mascot, quick answers in icon tiles and the email address in an ice box.
- **Privacy, Terms** (`Shared/LegalPage.vue`): a `LandingHero` with the last updated date in a soft white tag, then the sticky contents list on an ice card and the sections split by hairlines, with a “See also” line on ice at the end. `[[...]]` placeholders stay highlighted in amber until the owner fills them in.

## Article photos

Article photos are matched by slug in `resources/js/Components/Blog/guideImages.js`, with English and French slugs listed separately. The same photo is used on the card, the article and every teaser. A post without a photo gets a clean text card with an icon. Front matter `cover` is still the share image and wins when set.

## Honest content

Listings, counts and guides come from the server. Unknown salary, remote eligibility, relocation and visa stay unknown. Remote does not mean worldwide. Opening an outside link does not apply or send a CV. Do not invent testimonials, user counts, live activity, company verification or features. The photos are generated scenes, never real users, staff or partners.

## Behaviour

Content is visible without waiting for animations, and any motion stops under reduced motion. Keep keyboard focus visible, label every field, use native `<details>` for FAQs and the menus. Long French labels must wrap. Icons come from `@lucide/vue` or the shared `Icon` component.

## Authentication pages

Login, registration and the other account pages use `Guest.vue`: a navy story panel with line art, the label “Your next chapter”, a mascot and an uppercase “Just looking? Explore roles” link on the left, and a white form panel on the right (the navy panel becomes a navy header on phones). Fields are 52px with 10px corners, the submit button is a full-width `cta-primary`, and the role choice on sign-up is two 10px cards (cobalt border when picked). Their shared pieces are `Components/Auth/` (`Heading`, `Field`, `Feedback` and the class lists in `classes.js`). Group registration into account and profile details, with an explicit candidate/company choice. Keep inputs and buttons shadow-free, show field errors consistently, and never display demo credentials.
