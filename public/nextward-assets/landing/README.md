## Hero generation prompt

Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: hero illustration for Nextward, an internship platform connecting students with companies. Create one polished, contemporary 2D editorial illustration on a genuinely transparent background. Scene: a confident young North African woman with dark shoulder-length hair, wearing a cobalt blue sweater, cream trousers and blue sneakers, carrying a slim dark navy portfolio under one arm as she climbs three large simple ascending geometric steps towards a small four-point orange star. The steps are pale powder blue with dark navy thin line accents, perspective is charming and slightly isometric. A small terracotta potted plant sits beside the lowest step. Refined independent magazine illustration style: simple flat solid shapes, elegant slightly organic navy outlines, sophisticated restrained character proportions, minimal details, a tiny amount of print grain within solid shapes only. Palette limited to cobalt #2563eb, dark navy #0a1738, powder blue #dce8fc, warm cream #faf9f6, terracotta orange #e78955 and natural skin tone. Balanced composition roughly square, all subjects fully visible, fills the canvas with modest breathing space. Warm, optimistic, adult and professional. Absolutely no lettering, no labels, no cards or panels, no frame, no 3D rendering, no gradients, no glow, no drop shadows, no blurry edges, no floating interface widgets. Background completely transparent, crisp intentional silhouette, no cast ground shadow.

## Workspace generation prompt

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: wide background photograph for the middle employer section of Nextward internship landing page. Create a sophisticated editorial architectural photograph of a real-feeling contemporary Mediterranean creative studio in Tunis, a long oak shared desk receding from center toward the right, dark blue chairs, a laptop open with indistinct screen, notebooks, a restrained green plant, warm limestone walls, tall windows on the far right overlooking soft indistinct city roofs. No people. Late afternoon side light, understated natural materials. Wide cinematic landscape composition 3:2 or 16:9, empty dark wall and negative space in left 55 percent reserved for overlaid website text; most desk details in right half. Color palette deep midnight navy, warm stone and natural wood, muted cobalt accents. High-end independent design studio editorial photography, authentic not flashy. No text, no logos, no typography, no illustration, no frames, no interface elements. The website will apply a dark navy overlay so keep image atmosphere subtle and moody with enough detail in right half.
